01 · Three imaginary places

Station A raises the level, B increases the spread, and C increases the tail parameter. Reveal the stations one at a time. Each has its own copy of the same dashed reference; the axes stay fixed.

02 · Reuse the same quantities

The filled token first marks the changed parameter. Then all nine tokens become equally prominent: in practice, we estimate all three at every station. The next picture keeps the same A–B–C and L–S–T order.

These are design studies. The curves and matrix patterns remain illustrative; the station locations are fictional. Changing one parameter may change several distributional features. Different marginal distributions do not yet specify dependence.

The grid stays still

A deliberate simplification: keep each estimate's own uncertainty and drop the couplings.

On stage: point to one block. Click. Pause. Let the audience notice what changed before explaining it.
