01 · Three settings

The simple control shapes make “parameter” tangible. Reveal one curve and its label at a time. Keep the controls schematic; detailed instrument panels would add another object to explain.

02 · Reuse the same quantities

The labels become tokens. Add Station B, then C. Point across one row. The next picture uses the same A–B–C and L–S–T order.

These are design studies. The curves and matrix patterns remain illustrative; the station locations are fictional. The control pointers are metaphors, without numerical readings.

The grid stays still

A deliberate simplification: keep each estimate's own uncertainty and drop the couplings.

On stage: point to one block. Click. Pause. Let the audience notice what changed before explaining it.
